Uncovered Damages Due To Airbnb

You might think Airbnb insurance would make you whole after an incident with parties on a property listed on their website. Unfortunately, that is not the case.

Insurance denies all responsibility. They'll leave you with holes in your wall, holes in your car, and no car to get to work to pay for the repairs yourself.

Worst of all, they'll tell you go use your own insurance to cover the damages, which could cause insurance increases for years to come. The owner is really profiting off the neighborhood at our expense. The investment follows a pattern that has become all too common: A company makes an investment that manages to privatize the profits and socialize the losses. The host has lost little to nothing from the parties, but the neighbors have.
