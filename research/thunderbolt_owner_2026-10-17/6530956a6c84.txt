7. Discussion on Sign Requirements 
 
Town Administrator Milie stated it is political time.  If a sign is on town property or obviously in the right of way, 
i.e. middle of Victory Drive, Code Enforcement will remove them.  Council Member Henry stated he would not 
like to see uniform officers pulling signs but Code Enforcement should be enforcing the ordinance.  Council 
Member Crenshaw asked about state law for damaging signs or intimidation.  Town Attorney Barrow stated that is 
criminal not ordinance related.  Council Member Crenshaw brought up the difference between right of way and 
utilities easement.  Council Member Drohan stated the ordinance is broad on all kinds of signs.  He suggested the 
Council should do an amendment for political signs stating a number of feet off the street.  Town Attorney Barrow 
suggested if we are going to make this change it would not happen until after the election.  Mayor Goette stated it 
is not just political signs.  Council Member Crenshaw stated there have been complaints about signs and asked 
Council Member Barbaree to speak to one of the complaints.  Council Member Barbaree stated he was approached 
by a neighbor expressing concern about multiple signs in her yard.  The neighbor felt pressured to put another sign 
in the yard when she already had a sign from another candidate.  Jan Yarborough, 24 Bonaventure Road, and 
James Lavin, candidate for Council, asked if the Town had a sign ordinance.  Town Administrator Milie explained 
there seems to be some confusion on enforcement but there is an ordinance.  The second issue being touched on by 
Council is treatment of citizens putting signs in their yards; Council Member Crenshaw stated there have been 
complaints from businesses too.  Owners who do not live in the Town and do not want to get into the politics of 
Thunderbolt.  Council Member Ward stated any political candidate has to ask the citizens to put signs on their 
property, not on the right-a-way.  What has been happening is candidates are placing signs without permission.  
Council Member Williams stated everyone who has his sign has given permission.  Some of his signs have been 
moved, pulled and/or thrown away.  Council Member Crenshaw stated it is a misdemeanor to vandalize signs and 
is a fine of $1,000 per occurrence.

Chief Clayton was asked to summarize the Blue Line Contract currently being negotiated.  Chief Clayton explains 
there will be camaras for the school zone around Johnson High School.  If someone is going 11 or more over the

12. Legal Report – Town Attorney 
a) Second Reading of the Short-term Rental Ordinance 
 
Town Attorney Barrow stated this is the second reading.  There were some minor changes made to the draft 
ordinance.  On page 3 transient needs to be deleted, pg4 registration, top of page 5 recommendation of monthly to 
daily, change amount 100-150, page 6 change feed to fees. There are some blanks that need to still be filled.  
 
Council Member Crenshaw asked what the current number of STRs in Thunderbolt.  Town Administrator Milie 
stated 66 short term rentals from a host compliance company provided.  Town Administrator Milie suggested 
Council create zones and allow 10% more than 66.  Council Member Williams points out Thunderbolt was the 
only community had a net loss in population in Chatham County.   
 
Kailey Garrison, 2602 Dogwood, asked how the Town counted 66 STRs.  Town Administrator Milie explained 
through a host compliance company.  Ms. Garrison asked how would multiple STRs be counted on same parcel.  
Town Administrator Milie stated one per PIN. 
 
Jan Yarbrough, 24 Bonaventure Rd, asked were the 66 residents and condos?  Town Administrator Milie stated the 
host compliance came up with 66 different listings, no distinction of residents versus condos.  Currently the Town 
STRs is 5% of the residents. Council Member Williams stated some condo associations do not allow STRs.  Ms. 
Yarbrough stated if we do not have an ordinance all the residents could be short-term-rental.  We have learned 
from Tybee and Savannah there are trash issues, water usage and traffic.  Ms. Yarbrough recommended 
strengthening the process in the ordinance.  Council Member Crenshaw points out if everyone was a STR, we 
could not have Council.  Ms. Yarbrough suggested each STR has to provide a two scale parking drawing and 
though the ordinance references Good Neighbor Policy, they should have to comply. 
 
Geneva Golden, 3126 Gilbert St, asked how would you know if there are STR.  Town Administrator Milie stated 
there is no full proof way but the Town would look for those who are advertising.  Host Compliance take listed 
registration with one that pops up.   Council Member Crenshaw stated the ordinance requires a sign.  Council 
Member Barbaree stated there is a grey area where someone is just renting a room.  Council Member Crenshaw 
stated there is occupancy requirement in the ordinance. 
 
Ed Drohan III, 3319 Bannon Dr., stated he had no objection STR regulations.  He did suggest in regards to art. III 
sec 6-301 on the intent, promoting tourism should be added.  Mr. Drohan asked what is occupation tax? Clerk of 
Council Denion explained it is same as Business License.  Mr. Drohan stated he appreciated not using blight in the 
ordinance.  In his experience people have been respectful and responsible.   
 
James Lavin, 3125 Robertson Ave, asked what is the effect on Thunderbolt by allowing STR.  He has concerns 
with running out of water.  What is good for Thunderbolt?  If the intent of the ordinance is to identify them, he is 
not sure that will be effective.     
 
Della Lavin, 3125 Robertson Ave, stated her concerns come under the loophole.  What about those that do not use 
Air BB.  Do the STRs have to provide a booklet with rules?  Council Member Williams stated we could look at 
voter registration role and check those without.  Council Member Barbaree stated we could look at the homestead 
exemptions. 
 
Laurie DeVegter, 3212 Center St, stated Chatham County does require a parking layout and this ordinance should 
as well.  She suggested the staff look at tax revenue and stated it is smart to have a balance. 
 
Council Member Williams made the motion to table short term rentals until later date for more research to 
the questions brought up this evening with a second by Council Member Ward.  Council Member Drohan 
recused himself.  Motion passed unanimously. 

 
 
Council Member Williams asked Town Attorney Barrow if Council has considered limiting trucks on certain Town
