AGENDA HEARING 
 
Public has five minutes per person to ask questions or comment on any agenda item. 
Mayor Williams opened the public hearing up at 6 pm. 
 
Mayor Williams asked Interim Town Administrator Matthew Walker to explain the Planning & Zoning application 
before Council this evening. Mr. Walker explained the request was to add a water tap to the property in order to 
provide water to wash equipment, dumpsters, and to water plants that the applicant wished to plant along the front side 
of the property. Mr. Walker stated the request was recommended for approval with the conditions adding a catch-basin 
or other items to catch the water being used. The concern is the contaminated water will run off into the ditches, which 
is not permissible.  
 
Council Member Lavin asked Director Crosby about the potential water usage by the applicant. Director Crosby 
estimated between 5,000-15,000 gallons per month. Council Member Crenshaw mentioned the Abercorn Creek 
withdrawal and asked if we owed the City of Savannah for any water usage. Council Member Lavin noted that more 
usage will cost the Town more. Council Member Drohan stated it is 2.8 times higher cost to buy water rather than 
produce water. Dr. Drohan stated we need to think about the future and asked if we will ever be able to drill another 
well in future.  
 
Sue DeRosa, 3215 Bannon Dr, stated she taught environmental science and there are ways to reduce water usage. She 
stated shallow wells are not a way to think about conserving and reducing water usage. She also stated that water from 
roofs, A/Cs, and car washes can be caught in a cistern and recycled.  
 
Joan Hayes, 2801 Hayes Ave, spoke about the condition of Shell Rd between Martha and Whatley. She stated it is 
terrible and wants to know what can be done about it.  
 
Meredith Allen, 3220 Falligant Ave, stated that houses being converted to STRs impacts the feeling of community. 
She stated it is new people all the time and it impacts all neighbors. She would like the Town to look into creating 
zones where STRs are allowed.  
 
Jan Yarbrough, 24 Bonaventure Rd, asked the Town to re-look at the P&Z request and possibly approve with a limit 
on the water usage allowed.  
 
Joan Dickerson, 3204 Gragg St, asked about the Shell Rd STRs (Ashlyn Retreat). Interim Town Administrator stated 
the property owner has had STR Certificates for these houses since before the cap of 70 was reached. The houses are 
now complete and the homes are now being rented as STR properties.  
 
Beth Ferencsik, 18 Bonaventure Rd, mention the blind curves on Bonaventure Rd. and stated stop signs are needed.  
 

 
Catherine Clements, 3224 Cedar St, mentioned there are 2 STR units on her street. She stated there are sometimes up 
to 8 cars in driveways/yards. She stated she called and complained, and that she didn’t know who was staying there or 
how many people. She also stated she was almost hit on Whatley near Wright Ave.  
  
Mayor Williams closed the public comment at 6:37 pm.

Portfolio Strategy:
The Federal Open Market Committee
(FOMC) left the Federal Funds Target Range unchanged at 5.25% to
5.50% at the June 12th meeting. Recent economic data has suggested that
the economy is slowing. Employment and inflation data has started to
trend lower over the past couple months putting into question the timing
of when the FOMC will begin cutting rates. The market is currently
pricing in a 25 basis-point cut in interest rates as soon as the September
18 FOMC meeting. It is important to note that the market was wrong
earlier in the year when it had up to seven 25 basis-point cuts priced in. I
believe the FOMC remains in a data dependent posture however, the data
is beginning to support a change in monetary policy.
Nonfarm payrolls beat expectations for June coming in at +206k versus
expectations of +190k. The Unemployment Rate increased to 4.1% versus
4.0% in June. Inflation data for June was mixed. Consumer Price Index
(CPI) data trended lower, and Producer Price Index (PPI) data came in
higher than expectations.
Overnight General Collateral (GC) Repurchase Agreements averaged a
yield of 5.32% for June. Treasury Bill yields averaged 5.27% for 1-month
maturities, 5.31% for 3-month maturities, 5.28% for 6-month maturities
and 5.12% for 12-month maturities.
