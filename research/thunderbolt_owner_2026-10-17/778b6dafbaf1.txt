5. Nominations for Mayor Pro-Tem 
 
Mayor Williams asked for any nomination for Mayor Pro-tem.  Council Member Drohan stated this election was 
different than other elections.  There were six good folks running for 6 good positions as alderman. Generally, 
the strategy is to look at the person with the second highest number of votes to be the Mayor Pro-tem.  The way 
things worked out this year he would like to wave that particular procedure and would like to recommend David 

 
Crenshaw for the current term.  Council Member Drohan made the motion to nominate David Crenshaw as 
Mayor Pro-tem.  Council Member Ward seconded the motion.  It was open for discussion.  Town Administrator 
Milie asked Town Attorney Barrow to clarify if this is a practice or procedure.  Town Attorney Barrow explained it 
is not a procedure.  There is no legal requirement to follow a past practice that is not grounded in policy, or an 
ordinance.  Mayor Williams asked if there are any other nomination.  Hearing none, Mayor Williams asked for a 
vote.  The vote was four yes and two abstained, Council Member Lavin and Council Member Crenshaw.  The 
motion passed. 
 
6. Presentations: 
 
a) Atlantic Waste 
 
Ben Wall presented Mallory Boyd as the new point of contact. Ms. Boyd presented the changes that were 
made this past year.  There was a reduction to the number of days of collection.  This has allowed better 
service with less collected 230 tons of recycling. 81.5 tons of yard debris before April. Now, yard waste and 
trash are collected on the same day. There have been staffing struggles but there have been reduced 
numbers with turnover and more consistent staffing. Two drivers and route assistants have been hired.  
There has been a change with recycling. Atlantic Waste are currently using Attaway Recycling.   Town 
Administrator Milie asked if the modifications with recycling to limit the contamination resolved the issue.  
Ms. Boyd stated they have had success at removing the major contamination.  The trailer park was having 
issues with recycling properly. Their contamination was causing the entire Town’s recycling to be sent to the 
landfill.  The trailer park no longer received the recycling service.  Council Member Drohan asked about 
options for yard debris besides landfill.  Ben Watson stated it can go to a C & D facility but the current option 
produces gas. 
 
b) Axon Enterprise Inc.  
 
Ross Blank and Rob Murangelo with Axon Enterprise explained the Camara and Taser Program.  They 
explained the Taser 7 is more effective and safer for police and citizens.  Town Administrator Milie asked 
from a liability standpoint, the availability of less than lethal force, other than reducing law suits, what are 
the other highlights?  They explained there is a five year warranty.  The Taser 7 is a two-shot weapon versus 
the older models with one shot. The two shot weapon improves the proper deployment to reduce possible 
injuries.  The batteries are rechargeable.  There is no additional charge for batteries as the previous contract.  
There is an unlimited cartridge replacement as well.  They explained the camaras are upgraded at no cost.  
c) McGriff Insurance 
 
Dana Stevens – the Town’s Insurance Representative explained the Town has achieved renewal.  He works 
with Selective Insurance and Key Insurance to insure coverage for the town.  The program in place will do 
what it needs to do to insure the Town.  The new Taser and Camera Program the Police are using was helpful 
during the underwriting.  Town Administrator Milie asked if the Town is protected as possible, including the 
library new lease.  Mr. Stevens state yes.    Council Member Crenshaw asked what is covered under crime.  It 
was explained the crime coverage is build in to the insurance.  This is a 1st party coverage and does contain a 
cyber coverage to protect against ransomware.  Council Member Crenshaw asked about care insurance and 
employees using their own cars.  It was explained they are covered it is an at fault accident after their 
primary has maxed out.

e) IT & Cyber Security Plan 
 
Eric Fournier, with AI Technology Solutions, spoke on the Town’s IT and Cyber Security Plan.  He explained he 
has been working with the Town since 2009.  Mr. Fournier explained the 2022 goals.  He would like to 
physically control temperature in the server room.  Looking for a mini split system by a third party to improve 
the system.  He is going to work to extend the Wifi Coverage in Town Hall.  He explained there are two 
systems being used, QS1 for utility billing and CJT for Police and Court.  He is going to test back up to both 
those systems.  He is looking into a cloud backup for the Town, since it is currently housed onsite.  This is 
currently cost prohibitive and suggest Council starting putting it in the budget.  Council Member Crenshaw 
asked about security.  Mr. Fournier stated he would like to protect against Ransomware by adding a DNS 
security.  This will allow control of web traffic.  He will be able to monitor what sites employees are visiting.  
He would like to begin employee security awareness training.  Council Member Crenshaw asked if it is true 
most attacks are after hackers have been on the system for a long time.  Council Member Brooks asked what 
was the time frame for switching to the clouds.  Mr. Fournier explained there will not be a full transition.  The 
system is too big.  He is looking at a few key vendors that offer the encrypted email.  Council Member Ward 
asked about price.  Mr. Fournier stated about $1500 per year for 10 licenses, he thinks.  Mayor about linking 
the website with social media.  This will be a separate vendor and Mr. Fournier will tie everything together.

POLICE DEPARTMENT: 
 
During the month of January, we had a robbery at the Enmarket convenience store.
